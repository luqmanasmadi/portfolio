How I run client work

  1. Understand. Workshops and interviews to capture objectives, priorities and constraints.
  2. Brief. Requirements written as a clear brief, specification, wireframes and user flows the team can act on.
  3. Coordinate. Developers, technical teams and vendors aligned on one plan, with timelines across concurrent workstreams.
  4. Review. Deliverables checked for quality, accuracy and fit with the brief before they reach the client.
  5. Feedback and approval. Revisions logged, changes controlled, approvals recorded.
  6. Deliver. UAT, go-live and handover, with risks raised early and a recommended fix.
